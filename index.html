About CRT Effects

Why old anime looks better on CRT: CRT displays naturally blur adjacent pixels, smoothing out the harsh edges in low-resolution animation. The scanlines also add perceived detail.

Shadow Mask vs Aperture Grille: Consumer TVs use a shadow mask (dots), while professional monitors use aperture grille (stripes) for sharper images.
